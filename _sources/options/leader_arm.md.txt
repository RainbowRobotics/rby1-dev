# Leader Arm
 
The **Leader Arm** is a fully active robotic system, where all joints can move independently, unlike passive devices. Sharing the same joint configuration as the RB-Y1, it allows both task-level and joint-level mapping for teleoperation. Scaled to about 65% of the RB-Y1, the Leader Arm is designed for user convenience, making it suitable for operation in smaller environments.

<br>

<br>

With **redundant degrees** of freedom, the Leader Arm can perform identical tasks in various postures. For Leader Arm control, press and hold the button to move the Leader Arm in a gravity-compensated state, and release it to hold the current pose.

Mounted on a tripod, the system can be easily repositioned to control the robot from different perspectives and angles, providing flexibility in teleoperation setups.

<br>

```{image} ../_static/images/options/leader_arm/leader_arm4.jpg
   :alt: leader_arm4

   :class: white-bg
```

<br>

This robotic system also features an intuitive handle equipped with a **trigger** and a **button**. Pressing the button makes the robot follow the **Leader Arm** motion, and releasing the button engages a **position lock**, allowing precise control during teleoperation. The **trigger** is mapped to the gripper, making it easy to control.
