### ⚠️Preventing Damage to the Tool Flange Connector

- Ensure the connector is properly aligned with the keyway (groove) before insertion.

### ⚠️Gripper Power Specification (12V)

- The RB gripper operates on 12V power.
- Do not apply 24V through the tool flange when using the standard gripper, as this may cause permanent damage.

