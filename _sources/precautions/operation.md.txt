# Operation

## Precautions
<br>

### ⚠️Caution When Pressing Direct Teaching Buttons Simultaneously

- When the Control Manager State is Enabled and Control State is Idle (indicated by a Solid Blue Status LED), you can manually move the robot's arms using the Direct Teaching buttons.​

- Torso & Dual-Arm Manipulation: If you press the buttons on both wrists simultaneously, the Torso will also be unlocked and move along with the arms.​

### ⚠️Caution When Releasing the Torso Brake

- When releasing the torso brake using either the brake button or the Web UI, the robot may lose balance depending on its posture.

- This may result in unexpected movement or tipping over, especially if the center of gravity is not properly aligned.

### ⚠️Risk of Hand Injury During Leader Arm Operation

- When operating the leader arm, there is a risk of fingers or hands getting caught between joints.

- Keep hands clear of all joint spaces during operation.

- Always maintain a safe distance and avoid placing hands near moving parts while the robot is active.
