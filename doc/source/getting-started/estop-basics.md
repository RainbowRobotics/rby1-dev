# E‑Stop Basics

Purpose: understand how E‑Stop works on this platform.

## Concepts
- E‑Stop types: hardware button, panel E‑Stop, software stop (if provided).
- Effect: immediately inhibits motion and/or torque, depending on configuration.
- Recovery: clear E‑Stop, then re‑enable torque (per system policy).

## First‑use guidance
- Keep the E‑Stop within reach during all first tests.
- Verify that pressing E‑Stop halts motion before any teleop or navigation.
- Document the torque enable/disable sequence for your setup.
