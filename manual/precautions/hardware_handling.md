### ⚠️Preventing Damage to the Tool Flange Connector

- Ensure the connector is properly aligned with the keyway (groove) before insertion.

<div>

</div>

<div>

<img :src="$withBase('/images/saftey_and_precautions/hardware_handling/tf_connect2.jpg')" alt="" class="white-bg">

</div>

</div>

- Do not force the connection, as this may cause damage to the connector.

### ⚠️Gripper Power Specification (12V)

- The RB gripper operates on 12V power.
- Do not apply 24V through the tool flange when using the standard gripper, as this may cause permanent damage.

<img :src="$withBase('/images/saftey_and_precautions/hardware_handling/toolflange_power.png')" alt="" class="white-bg" style="width:100%">
