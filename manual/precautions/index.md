# Safety

Read these precautions before you operate RB-Y1. Keep the E-Stop within reach, and see [E-Stop Basics](../getting-started/estop-basics.md) for how to stop and recover the robot.

## Signal words

Each precaution starts with a signal word that tells you how serious the risk is.

::: danger WARNING
A hazard that could cause death or serious injury if you do not avoid it.
:::

::: warning CAUTION
A hazard that could cause minor or moderate injury if you do not avoid it.
:::

::: info NOTICE
A risk of damage to the robot or other equipment. No risk of injury.
:::

Each box says what the hazard is, what can happen, and how to avoid it.
